Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AMM Logistics LLC ("AMM Logistics," "we," "us," or "our"), an owner-operated motor carrier based in Frisco, Texas (MC-1536951), handles information in connection with our website and freight services.

1. Information We Collect

We operate a simple informational website with no contact forms. We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to view our site. We may receive information only when you choose to contact us directly:

  • Contact details you provide — such as your name, company, phone number, and email address when you call or email us about a load.
  • Freight and shipment details — information you share so we can quote, schedule, and complete a haul.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and arrange freight services;
  • Coordinate pickup, transit, and delivery of loads;
  • Maintain records required for regulatory and insurance purposes.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your information. We may share limited details only as needed to perform freight services (for example, with brokers, shippers, or receivers connected to your load) or where required by law, regulation, or to comply with our obligations as a DOT-regulated carrier.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices

Because we collect information only when you contact us directly, you control what you share. To request that we update or remove the contact information you have provided, reach out using the details below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business use and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
