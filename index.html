Our Services

What We Haul

Two trailer types, one standard of service. We move general freight for brokers who need it picked up, secured, and delivered without the follow-up calls.

Flatbed Freight

Open-deck hauling for oversized, building, and machinery loads — securely strapped, tarped, and run on schedule.

Box Truck Delivery

Enclosed, weather-protected transport for palletized and boxed freight — ideal for regional runs and final delivery.

General Freight

Dependable point-to-point freight across the country, with clear communication from dispatch to drop-off.

Who We Are

Industrial-Strength Logistics

AMM Logistics LLC is an owner-operated freight carrier based in Frisco, Texas, with two years moving general freight on flatbed and box trucks.
